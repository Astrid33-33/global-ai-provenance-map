Technical standards and implementation

The legal duty and the technical route are not the same thing. This comparison separates mandated outcomes from standards and voluntary implementation guidance.

Reading the comparison: China names a mandatory national standard; the EU binds providers to a technology-neutral outcome and offers a voluntary Code of Practice; California sets statutory criteria and detection-tool duties without prescribing one universal watermark format. California’s separate large-platform specifications duty is scheduled for 1 January 2027.

Three layers of a provenance record.

A sound review keeps legal obligations, provider claims, and observed files as distinct evidence.

Human-readable disclosure

Text labels, visible marks, audio cues, or interface notices that tell an audience content was generated or altered.

Machine-readable provenance

Metadata, content credentials, or other technical signals that systems can inspect and use for tracing.

Observed evidence

Original output files, metadata inspection, detector results, and screenshots. A policy promise is not a substitute for a file-level check.
